Art bible · checkpoint 1 · hero, whip & revolver

Relic Runner

A 90s cinematic platformer in the line of Prince of Persia, Flashback and Another World. A fedora-wearing adventurer runs, skids, swings on a bullwhip and fires a revolver aimed with the mouse. Every image on this page is rendered from the playable Godot build.

01 · The hero

Animation set

A polygon cut-out on a 14-part bone rig, about 32px tall in a 480×270 frame, shown here at 2×. Each clip is a few strong keyframes with Catmull-Rom easing, crossfaded in play. In the game the gun arm and head are layered on top and track the mouse. These strips show the authored arms.

24 fps render
02 · Feel

Controls

ADWalk. Walking away from the mouse is a backpedal.
ShiftHold to run. Stopping or reversing from a run skids.
SpaceJump. From a run, a long jump that clears 5 tiles.
WSClimb up or drop from a ledge. Reel the whip in or out.
LMBRevolver shot at the cursor. 6 rounds, ricochets off rock.
RMBWhip: swing on an anchor, yank a target, or crack.
RReload, one shell at a time.
MouseAttention. Head and gun follow it, and the body turns to it unless running.
03 · Biomes

Rocky canyon & jungle temple

Limited palettes with ordered (Bayer) dithering, lit from the upper left. Three parallax layers per biome. The rock is a 16-shape autotile set with two variants. Everything is generated by tools/gen_art.py.
