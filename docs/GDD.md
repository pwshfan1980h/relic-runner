# Relic Runner — game design (working title)

A 90s-style cinematic platformer in the vein of *Prince of Persia*, *Flashback* and
*Another World*, starring a fedora-wearing adventurer with a bullwhip and a revolver.

## Pillars
1. **Weighty, rotoscope-fluid movement.** Momentum matters: runs skid to a stop, turns
   pivot, a running jump clears gaps a standing jump can't. Ledges are grabbed and climbed.
2. **The whip is the traversal tool.** Right click lashes the whip: wrap an anchor and
   swing across chasms, or yank an enemy (or crate) toward you.
3. **The revolver is the problem solver.** Left click fires where you look. Six rounds, a
   deliberate reload, ricochets off rock.
4. **Attention follows the mouse.** Head and gun arm track the cursor; standing or walking,
   the hero turns to face it; running, the hero faces the way they're going and aims back.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| A / D | Walk (Shift held: run) |
| Space | Jump; while running, long jump; while swinging, let go |
| W | Climb a ledge you're hanging from; reel in the whip |
| S | Drop from a ledge; pay out the whip |
| Left click | Revolver shot toward the mouse |
| Right click | Whip: swing on an anchor / yank a whippable target / crack |
| R | Reload |

## Look
- 480×270 internal resolution, nearest-neighbour upscale, hero ~32px tall.
- Characters are **flat-shaded polygon cut-outs on a bone rig** (the Another World approach):
  2–3 tones per part, far-side limbs tinted darker, no outlines.
- Environments are **dithered, limited-palette** tiles and 3-layer parallax backdrops,
  generated by `tools/gen_art.py`.
- Lighting: `CanvasModulate` ambient per zone (daylight / cave), banded point lights for
  torches and muzzle flashes, tile occluders cast shadows.

## Hero animation set (all in `scripts/hero/hero_anims.gd`)
idle, idle fidget (hat tug), walk (and backpedal), run, skid, skid recovery, run-turn pivot,
standing jump, running long jump, fall, land, hard-landing roll, ledge hang, ledge climb,
whip swing, whip pull, reload, hurt, death. Aim and head-look are layered live on top.

## Enemies (3–5)
| Enemy | Biome | Behaviour | Whip | Revolver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scorpion | Canyon | Scuttles, tail-sting in close | Flip it over (stunned) | 1 shot |
| Rattlesnake | Canyon | Coils in pits, lunging strike | Yank out of its pit | 1 shot |
| Bandit gunman | Both | Takes cover, fires a rifle, reloads | Yank off ledges | 2 shots |
| Jaguar | Jungle | Stalks, pounces across gaps | Crack to scare it back | 3 shots |
| Stone Idol Guardian | Jungle | Slow, armoured; weak spot glows when it attacks | Can't be pulled | Only the weak spot |

## Biomes and maps (three per biome)
**Rocky dirt canyon:** ochre strata, sun haze, iron rings on chains, dead scrub.
1. *Dry Gulch*: tutorial for walking, running, skidding, jumping, the long jump and a first anchor; one scorpion.
2. *Rattler Mesa*: a vertical climb of ledge grabs and crumbling ledges over snake pits.
3. *Bandit Mine*: a dark mine lit by lanterns, with swings over the shafts and a bandit ambush.

**Jungle temple:** mossy block masonry, god rays, branch anchors, fireflies.
4. *Canopy Run*: a chain of canopy swings with a jaguar on the ground floor.
5. *Sunken Temple*: Prince of Persia traps (spikes, crumbling floors, timed gates, pressure plates).
6. *Idol Chamber*: the Guardian fight, then an escape swing sequence.

Maps are ASCII grids in `scripts/world/maps.gd` (16px tiles). Grab reach is 3 tiles and a
running long jump clears 5 tiles; design gaps and ledges around those numbers.

## Tech
Godot 4.7.2 (gl_compatibility), web export to GitHub Pages. Art and audio are generated by
`tools/gen_art.py` and `tools/gen_audio.py`. The play-through bot must pass at real speed:
`godot --headless --path . -- --bot`.
